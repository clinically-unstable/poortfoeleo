Pandamonium

TSA Competition Game Design Unity

Pandamonium is our TSA team's project for the Video Game Design competition.

It's a 16-bit style roguelike game, made in Unity. The character is a red panda, and you must traverse the map and beat enemies throughout it.

WASD - Movement

Space - Jump

E - Forward Dash

Left Mouse Button (LMB) - Secondary Attack/Fire Bamboo

Right Mouse Button (RMB) - Basic Attack/Forward Slash

Z - Next Dialogue

R - Interact/Talk/Continue Script

L-Show cursor
